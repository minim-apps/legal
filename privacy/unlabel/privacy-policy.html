unlabel — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

unlabel explains care, hazard, and disposal symbols found on product and clothing labels. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no personal information collected — an anonymous, invisible identity is created on your device the first time you open the app, solely to track how many explanations you have left.

Camera and photos

unlabel requests camera access to photograph a label, or photo-library access to pick an existing picture. The full photo never leaves your device. You choose one small area around a single symbol, and only that small crop is sent to our server to be identified.

Symbol identification

The small crop you select is sent to our backend, which forwards it to OpenAI's API to identify the symbol and generate an explanation. The crop is not stored — it exists only for the moment it takes to produce that one response, then it is discarded. It is never saved, logged, or used to train unlabel. OpenAI's own API terms govern how they process it on their end: https://openai.com/policies/api-data-usage-policies

Anonymous identity and credits

An anonymous identity (a random ID, no name or email) is created on first launch to track your remaining free and purchased explanations. This ID and your remaining balance are stored by Supabase, our backend provider. Their privacy policy applies to that processing: https://supabase.com/privacy

Purchases

Additional explanations can be purchased as a one-time, non-subscription purchase through Google Play's billing system. Payment details are handled entirely by Google Play — unlabel never sees or stores your payment information.

Analytics

unlabel does not use analytics.

Advertising

unlabel does not display advertisements.

Data deletion

Since unlabel doesn't collect any personal information, there's no profile to delete — uninstalling the app removes the anonymous identity from your device. If you'd like the associated anonymous record (remaining balance, no personal data) removed from our backend as well, email us at the address below.
