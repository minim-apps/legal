minim arte — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

minim arte is a collection of quiet drawing puzzles. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no personal information is collected. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. minim arte does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location, and it has no way to identify you.

Stored on your device

The app saves your progress — which puzzles you have finished and how far you are through the ones you haven't — along with your preferences, such as whether music is playing. This is stored only on your device, is never sent anywhere, and is removed when you uninstall the app.

New puzzles

minim arte ships with its puzzles built in and is fully playable offline. So that new ones can be added without waiting for an app update, the app periodically checks our content server for anything newly published and downloads it.

To do this, the app registers itself once on first launch and is given a random token, which it stores on your device and sends with each request so the server knows the request comes from a copy of the app. The token is not tied to you: registration sends only the platform name and the app's version number, and no account, device identifier, or advertising ID is involved. The requests download puzzles — they never upload your progress or anything else about how you play.

Analytics

minim arte does not use analytics, tracking, or crash reporting.

Advertising

minim arte does not display advertisements and does not share any data with advertisers.

Purchases

minim arte contains no purchases of any kind.

Children

minim arte is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Data deletion

There is nothing held about you to delete. Uninstalling minim arte removes your progress, preferences, and the install token from your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.
