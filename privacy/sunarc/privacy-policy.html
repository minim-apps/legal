SunArc — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

SunArc is designed to work without creating user accounts or collecting personal data.

Location

SunArc requests your device's location only to calculate and display the position of the sun relative to your current location.

Your location is processed on your device and is not stored or transmitted to our servers.

Map tiles

SunArc loads map tiles from MapTiler.

When requesting map tiles, your device communicates directly with MapTiler. Their privacy policy applies to those requests: https://www.maptiler.com/privacy-policy/

Analytics

SunArc does not use analytics.

Advertising

SunArc does not display advertisements.

Accounts

SunArc does not require an account.

Data storage

SunArc does not store personal information on external servers.
