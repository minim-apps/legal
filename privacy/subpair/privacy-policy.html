Subpair — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Subpair does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.

How translation works

Subpair reads the subtitle text that a video player displays on the page you are watching, translates it using Chrome's built-in on-device translator, and displays the result. This happens entirely inside your browser.

No subtitle text, viewing activity, or personal information is ever sent to us or to any third party. Subpair makes no network requests at all.

Settings storage

The only data Subpair stores is your own settings — whether it is switched on, your chosen source and target languages, and where the translated line is positioned.

These are saved using Chrome's built-in storage. If you have Chrome sync enabled, they sync with your Google account under your control. They contain no information about what you watch.

Analytics

Subpair does not use analytics or any tracking.

Advertising

Subpair does not display advertisements.

Accounts

Subpair does not require an account.

Servers

Subpair has no backend and no server component.
