asap — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

asap helps you find someone nearby to do something with, right now. Browsing is possible without an account; posting an activity, sending a request, or chatting requires signing in.

Account

Creating an account requires an email address and password. Before you sign in, the app opens an anonymous session so you can browse without providing any personal information.

Profile

Setting up a profile requires a first name, age, and one profile picture. Gender is optional, free-text. Your profile is visible to other users of the app wherever your name, age, or photo would normally appear (for example, on an activity you've posted).

Activities

When you post an activity, its type, optional description, and timing are visible to other users while the activity is active.

Messages

When you're matched with someone, messages you send in that chat are stored to enable the conversation. They are visible only to the two participants.

Location

When you post an activity, asap requests your device's location and attaches it to that activity so nearby users can find it and see an approximate distance. Location is only captured at the moment you post — asap does not track your location in the background or at any other time.

Data storage

Account, profile, activity, and message data is stored and processed by Supabase, our backend provider. Their privacy policy applies to that processing: https://supabase.com/privacy

Analytics

asap does not use analytics.

Advertising

asap does not display advertisements.

Data deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data (profile, activities, and messages) at any time by emailing us at the address below. We'll process the request promptly.
